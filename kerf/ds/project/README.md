# Kerf

Kerf is the AI front office for tree care companies. The brand has one job: make a tree company owner trust, in under five seconds, that this was built by someone who has stood in a bucket truck. Everything below serves that.

**Tagline:** Every call answered. Every quote chased.

## Name

A kerf is the width of material a saw blade removes. It is the one cut that decides whether the rest of the job is clean. Every arborist knows the word; nobody outside the trade does. Write it `kerf` (lowercase) in the wordmark and `Kerf` in prose. Never "KERF", never "Kerf AI".

## Voice

Tradesman-plain. Short sentences. Numbers over adjectives. Say removal, estimate, chipper, climb like someone who has done it. Banned: revolutionary, seamless, unlock, supercharge, game-changing, any emoji, any em dash. Headlines are statements, not questions. Every claim with a number carries its source in `eyebrow` mono beneath it.

Examples. Yes: "48% of home-services calls go unanswered. Yours are worth $1,800 each." No: "Unlock seamless growth with AI-powered customer engagement."

## Color

Dark is the default. The ground is `ink`, text is `bone`, secondary text is `mist`. Cards and bands sit on `pine` with a `hairline` border and no shadow.

The brand gradient, **kerf-cut**, is `sap` to `kerf` to `canopy` at 135 degrees. It appears in exactly three places: the logo mark, a radial `glow` behind the hero headline, and one headline word per page. It is never a flat fill behind text and never a button.

`bark` (amber) is the single primary call to action on a screen. One per screen. If two things are amber, neither is the call to action.

No blue, no purple, anywhere. Those are the colors every AI tool uses; Kerf is a trade brand.

Contrast on dark: bone 17.3:1, mist 11.4:1, sap 15.1:1, bark 9.2:1, kerf 7.1:1 (text 16px and up only). On light: ink on bone 16.6:1, the light-theme values of kerf, sap and bark are darkened so each clears 4.5:1 for text.

## Type

`display` family Sora for everything 24px and up, weights 600 and 700, tight tracking. `sans` family Inter for body. `mono` family JetBrains Mono for eyebrows (uppercase, +0.08em) and big stats. All three are hosted Google Fonts; load Sora 600/700, Inter 400/500/600, JetBrains Mono 500.

Hero headline uses `display` on desktop and `h1` on mobile with one word in the gradient (background-clip text). Lead paragraphs use `lead`, never `body`.

## Logo

The mark is a tree cross-section: an outer ring, an inner ring and a heart, with one diagonal kerf cut through all three at 38 degrees. The cut is the whole idea; never close it, never rotate it, never add a leaf.

Files in Logos: `kerf-mark.svg` (gradient, for dark grounds), `kerf-mark-mono.svg` (currentColor, for single-color uses, print, favicons), `kerf-wordmark.svg` (mark plus bone "kerf", for dark), `kerf-wordmark-dark-on-light.svg` (for light grounds). The wordmark text is set in Sora 700 at -0.045em tracking; in code, inline the mark SVG and render "kerf" as live text beside it so the font always loads.

Clear space: the ring thickness on all sides. Minimum mark size 24px. Minimum lockup width 96px. Never place the mark on `kerf` green or on the gradient.

## Shape and motion

Radii: `radius-md` on controls, `radius-lg` on cards and image frames, `radius-pill` on stat chips and the nav call to action. Borders are 1px `hairline`. Shadows only on the light theme (`shadow-light`).

Motion is 200ms ease-out. Hover lifts 2px. The hero glow drifts on a 20 second loop and nowhere else. Honor prefers-reduced-motion by removing the drift.

## Imagery

Real arborists, real trucks, real homeowners, at golden hour or dusk. Deep greens and amber in the frame. Software is shown only as abstract dark-mode blocks on a phone, never a readable screenshot. Never: stock office people, robots, circuit-board brains, handshake photos, or anything with another company's logo.

## Components

Stat chip: `radius-pill`, `pine` ground, `hairline` border, value in `stat` mono, source in `eyebrow` mono in `mist`.
Primary button: `bark` ground, `ink` text, `radius-md`, 14px 22px padding, `small` weight 600.
Secondary button: transparent, `hairline` border, `bone` text.
Pricing card: `pine`, `radius-lg`, the highlighted tier gets a 1px `kerf` border and a `sap` eyebrow "Most popular".

## Do not

Tie Kerf to any specific tree company. Use testimonials that do not exist. Use the gradient as a background. Use amber twice. Use blue.
