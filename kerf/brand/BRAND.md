# Kerf brand spec (v1, Nov 2026)

## Name and meaning
Kerf: the width of material a saw blade removes. The one cut that decides whether the job is clean. Pronounced "kerf". Always lowercase in the wordmark ("kerf"), capitalized in prose ("Kerf").

## Positioning
Kerf is a growth layer that sits on top of the CRM a tree care company already uses. It drafts the follow-ups, the estimate chases and the review asks, and the owner approves each one with a tap, so the business follows through and the owner can stay in the tree.
Tagline (primary): Your business follows through with Kerf.
Tagline (short): Follow-up that gets done.
One-liner for cold email: Built by a certified arborist who got tired of missing $3,000 removals while 60 feet up.

## Voice
Direct, tradesman-plain, zero hype. Short sentences. Numbers over adjectives. Never "revolutionary", "seamless", "unlock", "supercharge". Say "removal", "estimate", "chipper", "climb" like someone who has done it. No emojis. No em dashes.

## Palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| ink | #0A0F0D | Page background (dark), primary text on light |
| pine | #0B2A1F | Cards, section bands on dark |
| canopy | #0B5C44 | Gradient end, hover states |
| kerf | #1FB26A | Brand green, links, icons, gradient mid |
| sap | #C8F24D | Highlight, gradient start, key stats, focus ring |
| bark | #F2A43A | Accent for CTAs on dark only (one per screen) |
| bone | #F4F1EA | Light surface, primary text on dark |
| mist | #B9CFC4 | Secondary text on dark |
| ash | #5E6F68 | Secondary text on light, borders |

Gradient "kerf-cut": linear 135deg, sap #C8F24D 0% → kerf #1FB26A 45% → canopy #0B5C44 100%. Use on hero backdrops (as a glow, not a flat fill), the logo mark, and one headline word per page. Never blue or purple anywhere.

Contrast: bone on ink 17.3:1, mist on ink 11.4:1, sap on ink 15.1:1, ink on sap 15.1:1, ink on bone 16.6:1, bone on canopy 7.6:1, bark on ink 9.2:1. Kerf green #1FB26A on ink is 7.1:1 (ok for text 16px+); do not put bone text on kerf green (3.1:1, large text only).

## Typography
Display: Sora (Google Fonts), weights 600/700, tight tracking (-0.02em to -0.04em at hero sizes).
Body: Inter, 400/500/600. 
Numbers and labels: JetBrains Mono 500, uppercase tracking +0.08em for eyebrows.
Scale (desktop / mobile): display 72/44px, h1 56/36, h2 40/28, h3 24/20, body 18/16, small 14/13.

## Logo
Mark: a tree cross-section (two rings and a heart) with a single diagonal kerf cut through all rings at 38 degrees. Gradient fill on dark, mono (#0A0F0D or currentColor) when gradient is unavailable. Clear space = ring thickness on all sides. Minimum size 24px.
Lockup: mark + lowercase "kerf" in Sora 700, letter-spacing -0.045em. Files: kerf-mark.svg, kerf-mark-mono.svg, kerf-wordmark.svg (for dark), kerf-wordmark-dark-on-light.svg.

## Shape and motion
Radius: 10px controls, 18px cards, 999px pills. Borders 1px rgba(201,242,77,0.14) on dark. Shadows: none on dark; soft 0 20px 60px rgba(10,15,13,0.12) on light. Motion: 200ms ease-out, subtle translateY(2px) on hover, gradient glow drifts slowly (20s) in hero only. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

## Imagery
Real arborists, real trucks, golden hour and dusk, deep greens and amber. No stock-smiling office people, no robots, no circuit-brain illustrations. UI shown only as abstract dark-mode blocks.

## Do not
Tie to Saw and Order Tree Care in any way. No red/black courtroom theme. No fake reviews.
